# Cover design brief

## Concept

An editorial cover featuring a single decision sheet and a small path from alternatives to the chosen outcome and its evidence. The sheet has the visual character of a printed technical note. Use simple linework and generous margins so the diagram stays legible at thumbnail size.

## Composition

- Use **Architecture Decision Library** as the only prominent text. Place it in the upper left with room to breathe.
- Place the decision sheet in the lower right. Show a few thin horizontal lines on the sheet, two alternative paths entering from the left, and one selected path leading toward a small evidence marker.
- Use a warm off-white background, charcoal lettering, and a restrained teal accent for the selected path. Use solid and dotted lines in addition to color to distinguish the paths.
- Keep the title and the most legible part of the sheet within the center safe area. Create a landscape 1200 × 630 master and a 1080 × 1350 portrait composition that reflows the elements.

## Typography and production

Use a clear sans-serif title such as Instrument Sans with a medium or bold weight. Keep the paths and sheet shapes as original artwork based on the layout above. Export a high-contrast PNG or WebP. Review the title at thumbnail scale and verify the design uses the standalone product name throughout.

**Suggested alt text** — Architecture Decision Library cover showing two alternative paths feeding into a decision record with a linked evidence marker.
